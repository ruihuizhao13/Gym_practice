# Practice Gym · Tester guide

## Message to send each tester (before the session)

> Hi [name],
>
> Thank you for helping me test Practice Gym, a tool that helps new managers rehearse difficult feedback conversations with an AI employee and get coached on them.
>
> **When:** [date and time], about 60 minutes on Zoom: [Zoom link]
>
> **Your link:** [personal testing link — please don't share it]
>
> Please use a laptop or desktop with Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox. You don't need to create an account or install anything. When you open the link, you should see **AI connected** at the bottom right of the page.
>
> The scenario is fictional, so please don't type the names or private details of real colleagues. There are no right or wrong answers. I'm testing the tool, not you.
>
> Thank you!
> [your name]

## During the session (read aloud)

1. "Please share your screen and open your link. Check that it says **AI connected** at the bottom right."
2. "As you go, think out loud: say what you're looking at, what you expect, and what surprises you."
3. "Start on **Set up**. Keep the scenario **The late client report** and the **Warm-up** level, then click **Start with the 4-minute lesson**."
4. "Read the lesson and answer the check question."
5. "Now practice the conversation with Priya. Type your own replies. You can press **Hint** if you get stuck."
6. "When you're ready, click **End and get coaching**. Read your feedback, then ask the coach at least one question."
7. "Now click **Retry from turn …** and try that part again."

If a tester is stuck for more than 2 minutes, give one hint and note it.

>

> 2. What happened, and what would you practice next time?
